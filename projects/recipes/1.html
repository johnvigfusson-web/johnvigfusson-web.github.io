Butter Tarts

Background

This has much weaker backstory than the cookies but basically I wanted a canadian dessert and my dad told me about butter tarts so i decided to make my own recipe based on recipes I took online(it isnt the same recipe its just inspired) and I have improved the recipe over the past year and a half

Recipe

Ingredients

Messurement Ingredients
1.5 cup Flour
0.25 tsp Salt
3/4 c Butter
1 egg Egg yolk
1 egg Egg
2 tsp Vinegar
1/3 cup Cold water
1/2 cup Brown sugar
1/2 cup Dark corn syrup
1 tsp Vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl whisk the flour with the salt
  2. With a blender or knives cut 1/2 cup of butter into small cubes
  3. In a liquid measure whisk the egg yolk vinegar and cold water
  4. Put the cut up butter and flour salt mixture into a stand mixer, turn it to 2 or 3 speed a poor the liquid mixture into the mixer slowly
  5. After all is mixed up it into the fidge for 1 hour
  6. Mix together the brown sugar, corn syrup,egg, 1/2 cup of butter, vanilla, vinegar
  7. On a lightly floured surface roll out the dough and use a cookie cutter to cut it out and place the cut out dough into a muffen tray
  8. Fill the dough up to 2/3 with filling and sprinkle cinomon on top
  9. Heat the oven to 450 F and put the muffen tray in for 12 minutes
