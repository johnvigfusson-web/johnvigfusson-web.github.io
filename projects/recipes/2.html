Instructions

  1. preheat oven to 350 degrees F and set oven rack to middle
  2. next combine sugar, shortening, butter and molasses in a stand mixer with the beater attachnent on medium high speed until light and airy for about 2-3 minutes
  3. add eggs and vanilla to the stand mixer and mix until combined for about 20 seconds
  4. wisk the flour,baking soda, cream of tarter and salt in a separate large bowl
  5. remove the mixture form the stand mixer and add it to the flour mixture with a rubber spatula and fold it untill their are no pockets
  6. use a cookie scooper to put the cookie dough onto a parchment lined baking sheet
  7. place the sheet into the preheated oven on the middle rack and back for 10 to 13 minutes
messurement Ingredients
2 c white sugar
1 c vegetable shortening
1 c butter
1 Tbsp molasses
2 eggs
1 tsp vanilla extract
5 c flour
2 tsp baking soda
1 tsp cream of tartar
1 tsp salt
